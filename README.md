# Ranthambore National Park Safari Guide (Zones 1–10)

A quick reference guide for planning a tiger safari in **Ranthambore National Park, Sawai Madhopur, Rajasthan** — covering safari zones, vehicle types, and permit booking rules.

## Safari Zones Overview
- **Core Zones (Zones 1 to 5):** Prime tiger territory featuring lakes (Padam Talao, Rajbagh, Malik Talao), dense dry deciduous forest, and historic ruins.
- **Buffer Zones (Zones 6 to 10):** Scenic valleys, rocky terrain, and open grasslands with regular tiger and leopard sightings.

## Safari Vehicles & Timings
- **Maruti Gypsy (6-Seater):** Ideal for wildlife photographers, families, and private bookings.
- **Canter (20-Seater Open Bus):** Budget-friendly shared safari option across major zones.
- **Shifts:** Morning and Afternoon safaris (timings vary slightly by season between October and June).
